# Juxbly Brand and Trademark Policy

The Juxbly source code is licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0** ([`LICENSE`](LICENSE)). This document covers what the license deliberately does **not** cover: the **Juxbly name, logo, official domain, and official Chrome Web Store identity**.

The two are managed separately on purpose: the code ecosystem stays open, while the official Juxbly identity stays unambiguous.

## What is protected

- The name **"Juxbly"** and any confusingly similar spelling.
- The **Juxbly logo**, the water-drop / ripple mark, and the brand icon used as the Tool entry point.
- The official domain(s) and the official Chrome Web Store listing identity.

## What you may do

- Use, modify, and distribute the code under AGPLv3.
- Fork the project and develop it under a **different name and a different logo**, provided you:
  1. Remove or replace the Juxbly name, logo, and brand icon in the UI and store listing.
  2. Keep the AGPLv3 notices and state clearly that your build is not the official Juxbly.
  3. Do not imply endorsement by, or affiliation with, the Juxbly project.
- Write about Juxbly, review it, benchmark it, and use the name to refer to the project itself (nominative use).

## What you may not do

- Publish a fork, build, or derivative to a browser store under the name "Juxbly" (or a near-variant) without written permission.
- Use the Juxbly logo in a way that suggests your product is the official one.
- Register domains, social accounts, or store listings that trade on the Juxbly name.
- Remove or obscure attribution in order to present Juxbly's work as your own.

## Why this is not a "no forks" policy

Forks are expected and acceptable. The project's defence is not to forbid them; it is to keep iterating on the core, keep the official brand trustworthy, and keep the benchmark / recipe corpus as shared technical reputation. This policy exists so that "Juxbly" always means one specific, accountable thing.

## Contact

For permission requests or questions about this policy, open an issue or contact the maintainer through the repository.
